WHO ARE

BOYZnBUCK$?

A creative collective. The cultural zeitgeist of SA youth.

01

WHAT THEY DID

Fused streetwear, music, art & identity into a movement that redefined cool.

At their peak, they set fashion & musical trends like no other collective before them.

02

WHY THEY MATTER

They were part of the golden era of SA hip hop — early 2010s to pandemic — that paved the way for commercial success & brand identity.

03

WHY THEY FIT KFC

In skhotane culture, township youth competing in luxury fashion, the food considered expensive/status was KFC.

KFC has always been part of that flex.
